Privacy

Proview Reservations · last updated 23 November 2026

Proview Reservations is a booking system used by restaurants and other venues. This page explains what personal information the system holds, who is responsible for it and what you can do about it.

If you booked a table at a venue

The venue you booked with decides how your details are used and is responsible for them. Proview stores those details for the venue and only uses them to run the booking system.

  • What is held: your name, phone number, email address if you gave one, your bookings, and anything you or the venue wrote in the notes (which can include allergies or access needs).
  • What it is used for: making and managing your booking, contacting you about it, and looking after you on your visit.
  • Offers and news: only sent by the venue if you agreed. You can stop them at any time by replying STOP or contacting the venue.
  • How long it is kept: each venue chooses. The notice on the venue's booking page says what applies to you.
  • Your rights: you can ask the venue for a copy of your details, to correct them or to delete them. Please contact the venue first; they can do this directly in the system.

If you have a Proview login

For people who sign in to use the system (owners, managers and staff of a venue), Proview holds:

  • your email address, your role and the business you belong to;
  • a protected (hashed) copy of your password, and the times you signed in;
  • a record of changes made in your business, kept for up to 30 days so that devices stay in step.

This is used to give you access, keep the account secure and provide the service your business has signed up for.

The app keeps a working copy of a business's data in the browser of each signed-in device, and removes it when you sign out. It does not use advertising or analytics trackers. The booking page only connects to Google Maps if a guest presses "Show the map".

Personal information is not sold, and is not passed to anyone else for their own marketing.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your information, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to how it is used, and withdraw any consent you have given. If you are not happy with the answer you get, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
